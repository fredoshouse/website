An Introduction to Project Management

Welcome to Week 1 of Fredo’s Lessons, where I’ll be your guide on a journey into the fundamentals of project management. My goal is to equip you with the skills and knowledge to become a proficient project manager. No matter the nature of your work, we all must manage projects, and the concepts I will be introducing have value for everyone whether it be personally or professionally.

In addition to the concepts and terms I introduce, I will ensure the lessons feel practical and applicable by using first-hand examples from my extensive experience with managing projects.

🚀 Fredo’s Templates 🚀

As we progress in these lessons, I’ll introduce you to Fredo’s Templates, which will empower you to put these concepts into practice using the versatile project management tool, Notion.

So, What is Project Management?

While project management is a career in and of itself, it isn’t confined to a job title; it’s a valuable life skill that we often employ without realizing it. Think about it for a second—have you ever had friends over to your place for a dinner party? Congratulations, you’re already a project manager.

When you decide to host a dinner party, you’re essentially launching a project. You have a specific goal—to create a great vibe with a delicious meal that you will share with your friends. You make a mental checklist of what needs to be done, from choosing the menu (scope) and setting a date (timeline) to shopping for ingredients (resources) and sending out invites (communication). Even dealing with unexpected issues, like a surprise vegetarian guest, is part of the project manager’s job.

By recognizing these everyday scenarios as projects, you’ll start to appreciate that project management is something we do naturally. It’s about setting objectives, allocating resources, managing constraints, and adapting to change regardless of the nature of what you are working on.

Week by week we will delve deeper into concepts related to project management, so continue rocking with me and together we will explore art of project management.
