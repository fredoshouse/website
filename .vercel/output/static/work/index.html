What I do

growth

Ghost Note

I help the agency grow.

That means finding new opportunities, building relationships, talking to people, understanding what they need, and connecting the right people to the right work.

I like the relationship part most. I’m not really interested in selling someone something they don’t need.

I’d rather meet someone, understand what they’re trying to do, and see if there’s a reason we should work together.

operations

Integral

I help keep the machine moving.

There are usually a hundred little things happening at once — timelines, clients, teams, budgets, decisions, handoffs.

My job is often to look at all of it and figure out what needs to happen next.

I’m good at taking something that feels messy and making it feel manageable.

my corner

Fredo’s House

This is my little corner of the internet.

I write about work, growth, operations, agency life, money, relationships, ideas I’m thinking about, and whatever else I’m trying to figure out.

It’s less of a business and more of a place to think out loud.
