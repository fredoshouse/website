Hey, I’m Fredo.

Alfred on paper. Fredo to everyone else.

I spend most of my time doing two things: growth at Ghost Note and operations at Integral.

At Ghost Note, I work on the growth side of the business — finding new opportunities, meeting people, building relationships, and figuring out how to turn a good conversation into good work.

At Integral, I’m more behind the scenes. I help keep the work moving — clients, timelines, people, budgets, handoffs, and all the random stuff that has to happen for a team to do its best work.

Most weeks I’m somewhere between 10 and 20 different accounts at once.

It’s a lot. But I like being in the middle of things. I like figuring out what’s stuck, making the right connection, and getting people moving again.

I think it’s probably my African upbringing. Working hard is the only mode I was really taught.

But work isn’t the whole thing.

I’m also trying to build a life I actually enjoy. I’m in Seattle now, which means I’m trying to explore the city, meet good people, spend time with the people I care about, travel when I can, and have some fun in between all the work.

I’m still figuring out the balance.
