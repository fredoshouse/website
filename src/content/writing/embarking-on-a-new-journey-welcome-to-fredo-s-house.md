Peace Peace,

Ever find yourself deep in thought during a shower? That's been me lately. I'm all about consistency, but truly, when I first sat down to write this, I felt overwhelmed. I've been mulling over what I want this space to be—what message I want to share, what my brand should represent. And you know what? I got stuck. It's weird because my whole career has been about getting things done, but here I was, making excuses not to start.

So, I'm setting a clear goal: **I have about 200 subscribers now, and I want to grow that to 1,000. I need your help. If you could share this with five of your closest friends, it would mean the world to me. I'm looking to connect with people who get where I'm coming from.**

Here's what you can expect: some days I'll dive into account/project/operations management, other days I'll share snippets from my life, lessons I've learned, or reflections on experiences that are shaping my perspective. This past year has been transformative, and lately, I feel renewed—like I'm just getting started, almost as if I'm growing younger. It's an exciting feeling.

This space is all about **honesty**. I'm tired of endless texting and can't always call up my friends, but I can commit to giving you five minutes of my time to share what's happening in **Fredo's House**.

Thanks for taking the time to read this and for joining me on this journey.

Please share this with at least one other person and let me know what you think. I'm excited to use this platform as my new way of connecting. Eventually, I'll expand to other platforms—but for now, I'm about to hop on this call for new app build.

Peace,  
Fredo
