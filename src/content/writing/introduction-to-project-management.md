## So, What is Project Management?

![](https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/aca055d3-0e2c-4391-9748-04c2ac12f0ec/Screenshot_2023-09-06_at_5.01.36_PM.png)

While project management is a career in and of itself, it isn't confined to a job title; it's a valuable life skill that we often employ without realizing it. Think about it for a second—have you ever had friends over to your place for a dinner party? Congratulations, you're already a [project manager](https://www.coursera.org/articles/what-is-project-manager).

When you decide to host a dinner party, you're essentially launching a **project**. You have a specific **goal**—to create a great vibe with a delicious meal that you will share with your friends. You make a mental checklist of what needs to be done, from choosing the menu (**scope**) and setting a date (**timeline**) to shopping for ingredients (**resources**) and sending out invites (**communication**). Even dealing with unexpected issues, like a surprise vegetarian guest, is part of the project manager's job.

By recognizing these everyday scenarios as projects, you'll start to appreciate that [project management](https://www.pmi.org/about/learn-about-pmi/what-is-project-management) is something we do naturally. It's about setting objectives, allocating resources, managing constraints, and adapting to change regardless of the nature of what you are working on.
